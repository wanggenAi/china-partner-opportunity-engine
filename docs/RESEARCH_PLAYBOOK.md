# Research evidence

Prefer actual requests for help over provider advertisements. Explore supplier questions, hardware founder communities, China platform obstacles, narrow market research and local verification. Use concrete queries; broaden categories when a batch is dominated by sourcing advertisements.

For each candidate, verify the original author and publication timestamp. Store the time zone and method. Capture a brief excerpt, observed facts, inference and unknowns under `private/evidence/`; do not archive an entire page with unrelated personal contacts. A search summary is a discovery clue, not enough to qualify a lead.

Check current activity, resolution, deadlines, overseas identity and permitted contact channel. Never convert a relative date into an exact date without primary metadata. A recent post can still be resolved; a stale post needs a low-pressure current-need question. Duplicate URLs, authors with identical requests and cross-posts should be merged before outreach.

Separate three questions: does the need exist, can this founder perform a narrow useful task, and is paid cooperation plausible? Financing inventory, specialist engineering, safety approval and regulated claims are not assumed capabilities.

Evidence lives privately; `schemas/evidence.schema.json` is the public contract. `research/examples/` contains synthetic examples only.
