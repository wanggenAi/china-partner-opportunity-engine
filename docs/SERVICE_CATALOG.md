# Service catalog

| Small task | Verifiable result | Boundaries |
|---|---|---|
| One-supplier fact check | Named supplier, questions, original responses and open issues | No guarantee of factory ownership or capacity from a phone call |
| Narrow product/seller comparison | Up to three options against a buyer's written criteria | Prices and availability are time-stamped; no universal cheapest claim |
| Chinese website research | Annotated pages, translated relevant terms and unresolved facts | No legal advice or authority to act for the client |
| Sample information check | SKU, MOQ, sample terms, lead-time claim and return policy | No purchase until costs and authority are approved |
| Local coordination | Agreed contacts, progress log and evidence | Confirm city, availability and travel cost before offering a visit |
| Technical information clarification | Exact model/specification questions and supplier answers | No professional engineering, safety or certification sign-off |

Discuss a fixed fee for a bounded trial, an hourly cap for uncertain research, or a separately approved day rate plus expenses for local work. Product, shipping, third-party inspection and travel costs are separate. No hidden supplier kickbacks. All prices are proposals requiring founder approval; no public numeric rate is implied.
