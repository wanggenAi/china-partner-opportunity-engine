# Personal trust model

A credible China-side partner earns trust through useful work and evidence. Positioning must not imply a trading company, cheap sourcing intermediary, universal errand service or specialist credential.

- Problem solving: identify the missing decision, facts and possible checks.
- Research: compare public and Chinese-language sources; record provenance and uncertainty.
- Local execution: confirm whether a call, supplier contact, sample or site task is practical before offering it.
- Communication: report who was contacted, what was asked, what was answered, evidence received, unresolved points and the next check.
- Ownership: advance the agreed problem and flag blockers promptly.
- Honesty: label unverified claims and refer specialist questions to qualified people.

`why_me_for_this_lead` must connect selected strengths to one concrete task. A hardware need may justify technical understanding and structured documentation; a simple errand only needs location, understanding and a practical plan. A founder's technology background does not establish electronics engineering, factory audit or certification expertise.

A trust signal is a demonstrable work product: an annotated seller check, original responses, a source table or a clear acceptance checklist. Early work has a defined scope, result, exclusions and cost approval. Never fabricate a past report or client testimonial.
